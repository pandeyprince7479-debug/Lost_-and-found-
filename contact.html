Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to an item when it is found?

When an item is turned in, it is logged into our database, photographed, and stored securely in the Lost and Found office until its owner claims it.

How do you verify if the item belongs to the person asking for it?

To claim an item, you must provide a detailed description, point out unique features, or show proof of ownership, such as a photo or receipt.

What are the places where items are usually found?

Items are most commonly recovered from library study areas, campus cafeterias, classrooms, and common social spaces around the university.
